About ZeroPlate

About a third of all food produced is never eaten, while people nearby go hungry. ZeroPlate closes that gap at neighbourhood scale: providers post leftovers in seconds, NGOs see what is closest and still safe to collect, and every rescued meal is counted. Built for the Zero Food Waste hackathon challenge.
